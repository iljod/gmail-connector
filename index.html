Gmail Connector

Gmail Connector is a simple connector. It links your Google account to the agents you choose, so they can access your Gmail inbox on your behalf.

The connector does not read, store, index, or analyze your email or personal data. It only passes access through the Google OAuth flow. You stay in control and can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google and approve the requested Gmail permissions.
  2. Your agent uses the connection to call Gmail on your behalf.
  3. Revoke access at any time to disconnect.
