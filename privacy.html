Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Summary

Gmail Connector does not read, store, sell, or share your email or personal data. It is a connector only.

Data we collect

We do not collect your email content, contacts, attachments, or other personal data. We do not run analytics or tracking on this site.

Google user data

When you connect your Google account, Google issues an OAuth access token to the connector. The connector uses the token only to forward requests to the Gmail API for the agent you authorized. The connector does not write Google user data to disk or to any database, and does not use it for advertising, profiling, or model training.

Gmail Connector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further use of your Google data by the connector.

Third parties

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Your agent is a separate service that you choose and control; its handling of data is governed by its own policies.

Children

This service is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
